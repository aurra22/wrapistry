Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add gifts to your cart or describe your idea, fill in the form and open WhatsApp with your message. We will reply to confirm details.

Can I customize the items and colors?

Yes, tell us your preferred colors and items in the form. We will confirm what is possible.

Is a 50% advance required?

Yes. A 50% advance payment confirms your order. The remaining balance is arranged with you.

Which cities do you deliver to?

We deliver to selected cities in Pakistan. Please confirm your city, delivery charges and timeline before ordering.

How long does customization take?

It depends on the gift and how busy we are. We will tell you the timeline when we confirm your order.

Can I request an urgent order?

You can ask. Add your required date in the form and we will tell you honestly whether it is possible.

Can I include a personal message or photos?

Yes. Write your message in the form and send your photos in the WhatsApp chat.

What happens after I send my WhatsApp order?

We review your request and reply with availability, price, delivery details and payment instructions. The order is confirmed once the agreed 50% advance is paid.
